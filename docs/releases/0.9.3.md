## 3. Change summary

English beginner layer:

- **`START_HERE.md`** — copy-first beginner entry in English, mirroring the
  Chinese guide.
- **`PROMPTS.md`** — 15 English prompt cards.
- **`TROUBLESHOOTING.md`** — 13 English recovery scenarios.
- **English README links fixed** — it no longer sends readers to
  `START_HERE.zh.md`, `PROMPTS.zh.md`, or `TROUBLESHOOTING.zh.md`.
- **English DSH skill** — `templates/dsh-skill/SKILL.en.md`;
  `init-project.sh --lang en` installs it.
- **`init-project.sh`** now copies both English and Chinese beginner docs.

Also includes the previous unreleased Chinese `START_HERE.zh.md` rewrite:
first screen is one copy-paste prompt, not Git/terminal preparation.
