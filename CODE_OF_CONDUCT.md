# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can contribute, regardless of
experience, language, or background. Be patient with people who are learning.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about the work, kind about the person.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming a mistake.
- Give actionable feedback: what to change, and why.
- Respect that English and Chinese are both first-class here. Do not mock
  someone's wording; help improve it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination based on identity or experience level.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainers' scope decisions.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any project space.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to `<conduct@example.com>`. Maintainers will review and may
warn, remove content, or ban participation. Reports are handled privately.

This is a lightweight code of conduct, not legal advice. Projects that need a
full policy can adopt the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
